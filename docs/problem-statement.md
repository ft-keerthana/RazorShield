RAZORSHIELD PROBLEM STATEMENT

To stop the merchant losing money to fraud, returns and chargebacks

THE PROBLEM : 
Digital merchants face multiple forms of financial loss:

- Fraudulent transactions
- Return and refund abuse
- Payment disputes and chargebacks
- Coordinated abuse across multiple accounts, devices, or payment methods

The challenge is not simply detecting suspicious activity. Merchants must make decisions under uncertainty.

Blocking every suspicious transaction can reduce fraud but may also reject legitimate customers, resulting in lost revenue and poor customer experience. On the other hand, allowing every transaction increases exposure to fraud and financial loss.



THE CHALLENGE
Merchant risk exists across multiple dimensions and timescales.

A single transaction may appear suspicious because of:

- High transaction velocity
- Geographic or identity mismatches
- Unusual transaction behaviour
- New or unknown devices
- Historical account behaviour

However, some threats are only visible at a larger level:

- A sudden increase in suspicious transactions may indicate a fraud attack.
- Multiple accounts sharing devices or network signals may indicate a coordinated abuse ring.
- Repeated returns may indicate return abuse.
- Chargebacks require merchants to quickly gather and organize evidence.

These problems are often handled independently, making it difficult for merchants to obtain a unified understanding of risk.

SOLUTION :
RazorShield is an AI-powered merchant risk intelligence platform designed to detect, investigate, and respond to multiple forms of financial loss.

Instead of relying on a single fraud score, RazorShield combines:

- Rule-based risk signals for known fraud patterns
- Machine learning for complex behavioural patterns
- Anomaly detection for emerging fraud spikes
- Graph-based analysis for coordinated abuse networks
- AI-assisted evidence analysis for chargeback response

The goal is not to automatically block every suspicious event. RazorShield provides risk context and recommended actions that help merchants make better decisions while minimizing false positives.

SUCCESS CRITERIA
RazorShield aims to demonstrate:

1. Measurable fraud and abuse detection performance on held-out data.
2. Explainable risk decisions rather than opaque predictions.
3. A balance between fraud prevention and false-positive cost.
4. Detection of both individual and coordinated risk patterns.
5. An end-to-end workflow that a merchant could realistically use.
